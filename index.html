The Challenge

Why nutrition matters

Malnutrition affects 1 in 3 children in sub-Saharan Africa. Poor nutrition leads to stunted growth, reduced academic performance, and limited economic opportunity. Yet many schools lack resources to provide adequate meals.

The current model is broken: expensive imported foods, unpredictable supply chains, and outdated preparation methods make nutritious school meals inaccessible to most students in rural areas.

"Every child deserves access to nutritious meals that fuel their potential and dreams."
